Pro tools

Pop out and Remove

Both run on your phone. No photo is sent to a server.

Pop out

Lifts the person or object in a photo out of its frame, so it breaks over the border of the collage.

Remove

Brush over something you don't want in a photo, like a stranger or a sign, and Collagio fills the space in.

Recaps and projects

A month in one post, and nothing lost

The Month Recap template turns a month of photos into one post. Every change saves as you go, undo is always on screen, and finished projects reopen under Keep editing.

Questions

Good to know

Is Collagio free?

Yes. You can make and save collages and carousels for free with unlimited photos. Free saves carry a small Collagio mark. Collagio Pro removes the mark and unlocks every Pro-badged item, Pop out and Remove for $2.99 a month or $24.99 once.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photos and projects stay on your phone. Pop out and Remove run on the phone too. There is no account and no sign-in. The privacy policy lists the little that does leave the phone, such as crash reports.

What size are the saved slides?

Slides save as full-quality JPEGs, 1080 pixels wide: 1080 x 1350 for portrait, 1080 x 1080 for square and 1080 x 1920 for stories. Those are the sizes Instagram posts at.

Is there a free trial?

No. The free version is the trial, so nothing starts charging you later. Pro is a monthly plan you can cancel in Google Play, or a one-time purchase.

Is Collagio on iPhone?

Not at the moment. Collagio is an Android app and needs Android 8.0 or newer.
