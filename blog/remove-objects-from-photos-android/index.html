How to remove objects from photos on Android

A stranger in the background, a trash can, a power line through the sky. Your phone can paint them out. Here is how, and how to get a result nobody can spot.

By the Collagio team · November 25, 2026

Removing an object means two things: selecting it, then filling the gap with background that looks like it was always there. Phones now do both in a few seconds. The results depend less on the app than on what you ask it to remove.

The tools you may already have

  • Google Photos has Magic Eraser on many Android phones: open a photo, tap Edit, and look for it among the tools.
  • Samsung's Gallery app has Object eraser on Galaxy phones, among the photo editor's tools.

If one of these is on your phone, try it first for a single photo. If you are making a collage or a carousel anyway, it is quicker to clean the photo where you are building the post.

Removing objects in Collagio

Collagio's Remove PRO runs on your phone, so the photo never leaves it. You can open it from the home screen, or tap any photo in a collage and choose Remove. There are two ways to select:

  1. Tap to select. Collagio finds the people and objects in the photo. Tap the one you want gone and it is outlined for you. Best for people, pets and clear objects.
  2. Brush. Paint over the thing you want gone. Set the brush size to just a little wider than the object. Best for wires, signs, spots and small clutter.
  3. Tap Remove. Collagio fills the area from the background around it. If you don't like the result, cancel and try a smaller selection.

A very large area may take two passes. If Collagio says only the middle was removed, brush over what is left and tap Remove again.

Tips for a result nobody can spot

  • Include the shadow. A removed person who leaves a shadow behind is the most common giveaway.
  • Work in small pieces. Three small removals look better than one big one.
  • Simple backgrounds work best. Sky, sand, grass, water and plain walls fill cleanly. Brick, text, fences and faces are hard for every tool.
  • Don't remove what touches your subject. If a stranger's arm crosses your friend's shoulder, the fill has to invent part of your friend. Crop instead, or accept the arm.
  • Zoom in to check the edges of the filled area before you save.

When to crop instead

If the object is at the edge of the frame, cropping is faster and always looks real. In a collage you can do this without leaving the editor: drag and zoom the photo inside its cell until the object is out of view.

Questions people ask

Can I remove objects from photos on Android for free?

Often, yes. Google Photos has an eraser on many Android phones, and Samsung's Gallery has Object eraser on Galaxy phones. Collagio's Remove is part of Collagio Pro.

Do my photos get uploaded when I remove an object?

Not in Collagio: Remove runs on your phone and the photo never leaves it. Other apps may work differently, so check their privacy policy.

Why does a removed object leave a blur or smudge?

The area was large or the background behind it was complex. Remove it in smaller pieces, include its shadow, and go over what is left a second time.

Can I remove a person from a group photo?

Yes, if they are not overlapping the people you keep. Someone standing in front of another person is the hardest case for any eraser.
