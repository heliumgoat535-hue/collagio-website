Collagio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Collagio is made by Cyberlab Automation. Contact: cyberlab.automation@gmail.com

Your photos

Collagio reads the photos you choose so you can edit them. Your photos, and the projects you make, stay on your phone. They are never uploaded. Features such as Pop out and Remove run on your phone. Collagio saves your finished slides to your phone's gallery.

No account, no ads

Collagio has no sign-in and shows no ads.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives a crash report: the error, device model, Android version, app version and an installation ID. We use it only to fix bugs. It holds no photos and no personal details you entered.

Purchases

Collagio Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment; we never see your card. Superwall, the service that shows Collagio's upgrade screens, receives an anonymous ID, the screens shown and your purchase status, so the right screen appears and purchases unlock Pro.

Cut-out feature

Pop out uses Google's ML Kit, which Google Play services downloads to your phone once. ML Kit may send Google anonymous usage and performance statistics. Your photos are not sent.

Children

Collagio is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: cyberlab.automation@gmail.com
